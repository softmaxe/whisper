# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-16

Initial Whisper release, based on OpenWhispr 1.10.2 at commit `834a0771`.

- Self-hosted ASR and cleanup with custom endpoints, models, and editable prompts.
- Dictation shortcuts, automatic paste, and a menu bar icon toggle.
- Dictionary, Snippets, local History, and dictation Insights.
- Local audio/video uploads and batch transcription.
- Apple Silicon ZIP releases with checksums and Homebrew installation.
